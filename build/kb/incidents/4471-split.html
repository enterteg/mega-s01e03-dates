parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug the queued fix is for. It covers every report from the app, by email, and from booking-v2 forms.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this: the form needs its hint to follow the tenant’s locale. It covers form reports from tenants on booking-v3.

one-day: bookings move by one day

Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; one meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking was made. Month and day are not swapped here, so the parser fix will not help. These look like a time zone or daylight-saving bug and need their own investigation. The desk had already noted them (desk-notes.json).
